Take to the sky Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

日本語版はこちら（Japanese）

Reach High Studio (“we”) offers the smartphone game app “Take to the sky” (the “App”) as a personal hobby creative project. This policy explains how we handle your information in connection with the App.

In short

  • We do not collect your name, email address, postal address, or phone number for you to play the App.
  • There is no sign-in or account registration.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. We have no server of our own, and we cannot see that data.
  • The App has no ads and no in-app purchases. We also do not send play or usage data to ourselves or to any outside service for analytics.
  • We use information you send through the contact form — such as a reply email address, if you choose to provide one — only to respond and for the related purposes described below.

1. Information we do not collect from the App

When we provide the App, we do not collect the following from the App:

  1. Name, email address, postal address, phone number, or date of birth
  2. Location (such as GPS), contacts, photos, or microphone or camera information
  3. Advertising identifiers (such as an advertising ID)
  4. An ID for sign-in or account registration (the App has no such feature)
  5. Play data such as game progress (as explained in “2. How game data is stored,” this stays on your device, unless you write and send it yourself in an inquiry)

The App does not include any third-party analytics or advertising software.

2. How game data is stored

  1. Play data in the App (such as stage progress, the state of coins and hearts, and settings) is stored on your device.
  2. This data is not sent to a server of ours. We cannot see it.
  3. If you delete the App, or if your device is lost or broken, this play data may be lost.

3. Advertising, purchases, and analytics

  1. The App has no ads. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not show a tracking-permission prompt.
  2. The App has no in-app purchases.
  3. The App does not send play activity or device information to us or to any outside service for analytics.

4. Information we receive when you contact us

  1. If you contact us through the contact form, we receive your message, the App version number (which may be filled in advance), and, if you choose to enter them, a reply email address and your name. The same applies if you reply to an email from us. This information may be personal information.
  2. We use this information only for the following purposes. We do not use it for advertising or for any other purpose.
    • Responding to your inquiry
    • Investigating and fixing problems
    • Responding to requests such as “please tell me,” “please correct this,” or “please delete this” (access, correction, deletion, and similar requests)
  3. We delete this information from the Google spreadsheet and from the email used for notices and replies when one year has passed after we finish handling the inquiry.
  4. We use Google Forms, provided by Google LLC, to receive inquiries. Responses are stored in Google Sheets and a notice is sent to an email address that we do not publish. This information may be stored on Google’s servers outside Japan (mainly in the United States).

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share information we receive through inquiries with third parties, except in the following cases:

  1. When required by law
  2. When necessary to protect someone’s life, body, or property, and it is difficult to obtain the person’s consent
  3. Other cases permitted by law

As described above, we use Google’s services to receive, store, and be notified of inquiries.

6. Security measures

We take the following measures for information we receive through inquiries.

  1. We manage the contact form’s admin pages, the Google spreadsheet, and the notification inbox with an account only we can use, and we turn on two-step verification.
  2. We delete information once one year has passed after we finish handling the inquiry.
  3. Use of services outside Japan: Inquiry information is stored using services of Google LLC (United States). The United States does not have a single federal law that, like Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information, covers all sectors; personal information is protected by state laws and by laws that apply to particular sectors. There are also legal mechanisms that can require a business to provide information to a government agency. We use Google’s services with this in mind.

7. Users who are minors

If you are a minor, please use the App after checking with a parent or other legal guardian. If you send an inquiry, you must obtain their consent first and write it together with them.

8. Requests for access, correction, or deletion

If you ask us to tell you about, correct, or delete inquiry information we have received, we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. Please use the contact form. After we confirm, based on what you submitted, that the request is from you, we will reply by email to the address you entered. If you did not enter an email address, we may be unable to reply. There is no fee.

9. Changes to this policy

  1. We may change this policy to reflect changes in the law or in the App’s features (including how data is stored, adding analytics or advertising, or adding countries or regions where the App is available).
  2. If we make an important change, we will let you know in the App or on our website before the change takes effect. If we significantly change the types of information we collect or how we use it, we will obtain your consent when the law requires it.

10. Provider and contact

  • Provider: Reach High Studio
  • Contact for inquiries, complaints, and requests such as access or deletion: the contact form (we reply by email to the address you enter)

This policy takes effect on November 10, 2026.
